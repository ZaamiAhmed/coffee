Our Story

Rooted in Nairobi, crafting globally.

The Nairobi Bean

Milgo Coffee originated right here in the bustling heart of Nairobi, specifically in the eclectic neighborhood of Kilimani. Our founding mission was simple: harness the rich agricultural heritage of Kenyan soil, master the art of the pour, and serve the perfect cup of coffee in a modern space.

We work directly with local farmers across Kenya to source the finest single-origin beans, ensuring fair trade practices while delivering a vibrant, unique flavor profile that truly represents our homeland.

Visit Us in Kilimani

Our flagship store offers a relaxing atmosphere away from the noise. Come by to pick up your online order or stay a while.
